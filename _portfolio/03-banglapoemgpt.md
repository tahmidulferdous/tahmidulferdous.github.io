A ~10M-parameter GPT-1-style decoder written from first principles (attention, positional encoding, training loop, sampling) for Bengali poetry.

Character-level tokenisation over a ~3M-token corpus, trained on a Kaggle P100. Best validation loss 1.693.

**Code and runs:** [bangla-gpt-from-scratch](https://github.com/tahmidulferdous/bangla-gpt-from-scratch)
